Support and account deletion

Last updated · 2026-09-29

Food remains on your device/iCloud. Account-deletion automation is awaiting deployment; requests are currently accepted.

Contact support

Email jsh097610@gmail.com with your language, app version, approximate time and a description. For point/purchase issues, include the account ID shown in Settings. Never send passwords, verification codes or card numbers.

Delete an account

Currently, Settings → My information accepts account-deletion requests. Automation that deletes the account, points and decoration ownership and tracks external providers is still awaiting deployment. A submitted request is not reported as completed deletion. Email support for progress or if the app is inaccessible. Anonymous accounts are included. Necessary Apple reauthentication and payment-evidence checks apply. Food remains on your device/iCloud; account deletion is not a refund.

Payments and ad rewards

Check App Store purchases through your Apple account. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If purchased points are missing, reopen the app and allow server verification to finish. Ad rewards require server confirmation and may be delayed. If they remain missing, send your account ID and viewing time.

Food and iCloud

Food records remain after logout or account deletion. Remove them directly in the app. With sync enabled, removal may affect other devices. For sync problems, check that devices use the same Apple account and that iCloud is enabled.
