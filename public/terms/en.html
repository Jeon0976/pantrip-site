3. Food records and checks

Check the dates, photos, barcode lookup results, and recognized dates you enter, and correct them when needed. App records and reminders do not determine whether food is safe to eat. Check the package dates, storage instructions, and the food's actual condition. You retain rights to the photos and records you add. If you enable sync, they are stored, transmitted, and displayed as needed to provide the service.

4. Device storage and optional sync

Food records and photos are stored on your device by default. After Apple or Google sign-in, you can separately consent to syncing them with the server and other signed-in devices. Signing in alone does not enable food sync. Turning sync off stops further uploads but does not immediately delete server copies. Deleting food while sync is on may also affect other connected devices. The Privacy Policy explains processing and deletion.
